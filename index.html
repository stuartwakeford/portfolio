Your product design partner

I’m Stu, a senior product design & strategy consultant with over 15 years’ experience delivering standout design solutions that balance user needs and business goals.

I've built deep domain knowledge across multiple sectors within startups, scale-ups, agencies and large enterprise, and I've started and exited my own ventures.

I use design to bring ideas to life and provide teams with a north star.

I partner with product owners to shape strategy, test ideas, and document detail.

I craft intuitive UX, elegant interfaces, and interactive prototypes.

I iterate at speed and bake user testing into my process.

I collaborate with developers to de-risk build and ensure quality.

I build and direct design teams and establish standards.
